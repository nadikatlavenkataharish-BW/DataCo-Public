# Retail — SAC custom widgets

> Proprietary — © 2026 The Data Co (Pty) Ltd. All rights reserved. See [LICENSE](../../LICENSE).

Retail performance and merchandise planning: store productivity, range classification, stock health and season sell-through. Currency switch: ZAR / USD.

| Preview | Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| <a href="Store%20Quadrant"><img src="Store%20Quadrant/Example.png" width="260" alt="Store Performance Quadrant"/></a> | **[Store Performance Quadrant](Store%20Quadrant)** | Stores on two KPIs (e.g. sales/m² vs margin %) in four labelled quadrants, bubble = revenue |
| <a href="ABC-XYZ%20Matrix"><img src="ABC-XYZ%20Matrix/Example.png" width="260" alt="ABC/XYZ Matrix"/></a> | **[ABC/XYZ Matrix](ABC-XYZ%20Matrix)** | Revenue contribution × demand variability 3×3 matrix with stock policy hints |
| <a href="Weeks%20of%20Cover"><img src="Weeks%20of%20Cover/Example.png" width="260" alt="Weeks of Cover"/></a> | **[Weeks of Cover](Weeks%20of%20Cover)** | Weeks of cover vs target band, with stock value, OOS % and aged stock % |
| <a href="Sell-through%20Curve"><img src="Sell-through%20Curve/Example.png" width="260" alt="Sell-through Curve"/></a> | **[Sell-through Curve](Sell-through%20Curve)** | Cumulative sell-through % by week since launch vs plan and target |
